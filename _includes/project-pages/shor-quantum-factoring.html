01

The arithmetic inside Shor

Shor finds factors by looking for a repeating pattern in powers taken modulo the RSA number. Computing those powers takes most of the work. In Gidney’s low-qubit circuit, lookups and a residue-cleanup stage account for about 84% of the Toffoli gates.

The same input bits select a row in several tables, one for each small prime. We pack their answers into a wider row, so a single lookup returns them together. In a controlled comparison across three primes, sharing alone reduced the estimated Toffoli count by 30.7%.

We retain multiplication inputs so cleanup can subtract the same terms instead of rebuilding residues. This lets intermediate residues grow without per-step modular reductions. The paper proves that the core transformations preserve the output and relative quantum phases when the registers are wide enough.

The revised circuits share a lookup across up to 37 primes. They compress the discrete-log registers, merge measurement-based phase corrections and feed the last multiplication directly into the Chinese-remainder accumulator. The benchmark checks the fused update against its allowed output tolerance.

The serial reference receives the arithmetic refinements that apply to a single prime, including direct table writes when a register starts at zero and the result depends only on the lookup address.

05

Fewer gates, but how much faster?

A quantum computer also has to move data, correct errors, and supply resources for gates. Sharing a lookup reduces the gate count but makes its output wider. How much time that saves depends on what the machine can handle efficiently.

Surface code · 16.2 hours at 3.18M qubits

The 1,450-logical-qubit circuit takes 2.66 days on 997,192 physical qubits versus 3.10 days for the reconstructed reference. The 2,925-logical-qubit circuit takes 16.2 hours with a larger processor. The smallest projected spacetime is 1.84 million qubit-days at 1,861 logical qubits.

Neutral atoms · two processor trade-offs

Within the published 1,480-logical-qubit memory, the best balanced-processor candidate takes 29.3 years versus 25.4 for its tuned reference. The best space-efficient candidate takes 91.7 years versus 109.5.

What the work adds to Shor

Shared lookups, retained inputs and a fused final accumulator update lower the estimated cost of Shor’s residue arithmetic. The surface-code model selects three different circuits depending on whether the objective is gate count, spacetime or runtime. The estimates use a sampled arithmetic benchmark and published hardware models. Compiling each design for its target architecture would test the effects of movement and scheduling.

What the circuit keeps

Pricing takes the expected discounted payoff over market paths. With equally spaced factor indices and fixed model coefficients, cumulative log returns can be recovered from running integer sums. The circuit also carries the contract history that determines what gets paid.

3 assets · 20 dates

Worst-of autocallable

The contract can settle early on a call date. If it survives after any asset breaches the barrier, the terminal payoff can include a loss based on the worst performer at maturity.

The factor-sum circuit keeps three exact 10-bit index sums and the call and knock-in records. It builds temporary integer forms for the date tests. Hardening stores signed differences from the barrier and checks every asset at each date.

1 factor · 26 dates

Target accrual redemption forward

The TARF accumulates signed payments until a knock-out or an accrual target stops it. The target caps the last payment, so the amount depends on what has already accrued.

The windowed circuit keeps a compact index, a shifted accrual, and a discount correction. Date-dependent lookups supply payments, and one cap-date calculation adjusts the final payoff.

What the checks establish

Coherent integer action

The factor-sum and hardened autocallable circuits implement their integer specifications for every input, incoming accumulator, and measurement record. The proofs cover phase cancellation, preservation of the path, and restoration of the workspace. The logical inverse uses the same Toffoli count and peak qubits.

For HHL, the Booth-polynomial circuit and two signed-Booth variants have all-input logical proofs. Their angle errors were checked numerically on all 2,031,616 inputs. The 987-Toffoli circuit’s maximum observed error was about 9.90 × 10−8 radians, within the 10−7 target.

Agreement with the financial payoff

Numerical lattice calculations estimate angle-tolerance violation probabilities of 6.248 × 10−8 for factor-sum and 3.56 × 10−8 for the hardened circuit. Their signed price biases are −2.625 × 10−6 and +1.01 × 10−5 contract units under the specified loading law.

On the 900 autocallable threshold stress cases, violations fell from 241 to 6 with hardening. The lowest-product circuit had 520 violations on those same cases. These fixtures deliberately probe the boundary.

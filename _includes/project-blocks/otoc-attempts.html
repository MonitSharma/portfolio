Two attempts, one format

{% if include.current == 'duke-quantum' %}A month later: 1,426 → 2,560{% else %}A month earlier: 1,426 at Duke{% endif %}

The Duke Quantum Hackathon (October) and the Midwest Quantum Hackathon (November) used the same BlueQubit OTOC challenge format: three open samples and four hidden circuit families, scored out of 3,000. The circuits are each event’s own. Between them, I moved Pauli-path runs to the GPU, turned on the simulator’s heaviest pre-processing (transpilation level 3), and checked every setting on look-alike circuits with known answers first.
